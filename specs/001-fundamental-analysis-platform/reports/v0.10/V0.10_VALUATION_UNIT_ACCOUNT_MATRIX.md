# Matriz de unidad de cuenta y compatibilidad de valuación — v0.10

| Métrica | Numerador | Denominador | Equity/shares basis | Fundamental scope | Estado si incompatible |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Market cap | suma precio×shares de todas las clases económicas | n/a | issuer total, ex treasury | n/a | blocked/unavailable/unsupported |
| EV | market cap total + deuda − caja | n/a | issuer total | consolidated | blocked/unavailable |
| P/E | market cap total | NI TTM atribuible parent/common | issuer total | parent attributable | blocked; NI<=0 not_meaningful |
| Earnings yield | NI atribuible | market cap total | issuer total | parent attributable | blocked/unavailable |
| P/S | market cap total | revenue TTM consolidado | issuer total | consolidated | blocked; revenue<=0 not_meaningful |
| P/B | market cap total | equity atribuible parent | issuer total | parent attributable | blocked/unavailable; equity<=0 not_meaningful |
| EV/Sales | EV emisor | revenue consolidado | issuer total dentro EV | consolidated | blocked/unavailable |
| EV/EBITDA | EV emisor | EBITDA consolidado | issuer total dentro EV | consolidated | blocked/unavailable; EBITDA<=0 not_meaningful |
| FCF yield | FCF consolidado | market cap total | issuer total | consolidated | blocked/unavailable |

## Escenarios

| Escenario | Decisión |
|---|---|
| clase única completa | permitido |
| multiclase completa | permitido sumando clases no solapadas |
| multiclase incompleta | solo descriptivo; valuation unavailable |
| ADR ratio conocido | convertir a subyacente y aplicar totalidad; sin FX |
| ADR ratio desconocido | valuation blocked |
| CEDEAR mapeado/ratio/moneda compatibles | compatible_with_limitations |
| CEDEAR no mapeado | valuation unsupported |
| listing ambiguo | valuation blocked |
| moneda distinta | valuation blocked |
| shares 91–120 días | limitación + cap |
| shares >120 días | unavailable |
| shares posteriores a precio | blocked |
| treasury no reconciliado | unavailable |
| split conocido y bases alineadas | permitido |
| ajuste desconocido | blocked cuando afecta base |
